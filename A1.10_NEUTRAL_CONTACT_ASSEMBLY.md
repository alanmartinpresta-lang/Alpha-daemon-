- physically resolved object-object contact;
- collision impulse / separation;
- a neutral adhesion state derived only from friction and relative motion;
- attached objects share momentum when physically adhered;
- persistence of object attachment through checkpoint serialization.

Retained:
- anonymous materials;
- mass, rigidity, friction, conductivity;
- push, grasp, release;
- no recipes, object names, tool concepts, construction reward, or construction goal.

Thermal model:
- uses bounded effective heat capacity and stronger homeostatic relaxation from the
  validated Genesis branch.

  310.17–312.03 K.
- checkpoint round-trip: PASS.
- direct physical contact regression: two high-friction objects attached and shared
  velocity without an assembly command.
