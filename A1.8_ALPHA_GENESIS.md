# A1.8 — Alpha Genesis

## Operational role
A1 is now treated as a **single autonomous Alpha** to observe over a long life.

No human task, scenario, target technology, construction objective, or survival
instruction is supplied.

The experiment observes whatever follows from the current AIRE world,
body, sensors, effectors, metabolism, homeostasis, learning and memory.

## What is protected
- No external action commands.
- No injected goals.
- No scripted behavior.
- Exact persistent checkpoints.
- Append-only life log.
- Death is an allowed experimental outcome.
- Recovery/injury and environmental events remain consequences of the world.

## What this does NOT claim
A1.8 does not yet guarantee spontaneous invention or construction. Those require
physical object manipulation/assembly capabilities in the current world.
